DECEMBER PROJECT

Defuse the Stink Alarm

MISSION

Each of 6 teams builds a timed alarm circuit. Another team must trace the system and disable the correct alarm path before time expires.

REQUIRED BUILD

  • Power source
  • Breadboard + jumper wires
  • LED indicator(s)
  • Buzzer/alarm output
  • Switch or button input
  • Correct defuse connection
  • At least 2 decoy connections
  • Student circuit diagram + secret key

DEFUSAL RULES

  • No cutting wires.
  • One wire move at a time.
  • Explain your reasoning before changing a connection.
  • Wrong-wire penalty may remove time.
  • Teacher verifies every build before tournament play.
